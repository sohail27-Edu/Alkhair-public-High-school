Admission Information

Al-Khair Public High School Umary welcomes applications for KG to 10th class.

KG to 10th class

Admissions are open across all classes offered by the school, from Kindergarten through 10th class. Parents are welcome to contact the school office for guidance on the right class placement for their child.

HOW IT WORKS

Admission Process

1

Fill the admission application

Complete the application form below, on this website, or obtain a paper copy from the school office.

2

Provide student & guardian information

Fill in accurate details for the student and the parent/guardian.

3

Attach required documents

Prepare a passport-size photograph, the school leaving certificate (if applicable) and any other relevant documents.

4

Submit to the school

Print/download the completed application and submit it to Al-Khair Public High School Umary along with the documents.

5

Application review

The school administration reviews the submitted application.

6

Admission decision

The admission decision is communicated by the school.
