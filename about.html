ABOUT THE SCHOOL

Knowledge joined with character

The story, philosophy, vision and mission of Al-Khair Public High School Umary.

OUR STORY

A school rooted in the community of Umary Mamund

Al-Khair Public High School Umary was established in the late 1990s and has served the local community in Umary Mamund, District Bajaur. The school was founded by Bahader Said, who continues to serve as Principal.

Since its founding, the school has offered education from KG to 10th class, working from a simple but firm belief: that a school's duty is to shape capable, honest and responsible human beings — not only to prepare students for exams.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Education is preparation for life, not merely examinations

The school believes education is more than obtaining a certificate. True education develops the whole person — joining knowledge with wisdom, honesty and responsibility.

Knowledge & Honesty

Meaningful learning built on truthfulness and integrity in every subject.

Character & Discipline

Strong character formed through discipline, respect and daily conduct.

Logical & Critical Thinking

Students learn to reason and question rather than simply memorize.

Imagination & Creativity

Space to imagine, create and approach problems from new angles.

Communication

Confidence to express ideas clearly and respectfully.

Wisdom & Responsibility

Sound judgement and a sense of duty toward others and society.

Leadership

Preparing students who can guide and serve — to “go and lead.”

Responsible Citizenship

Respect for community, country and humanity at large.

VISION

Our Vision

“To become a school that develops knowledgeable, honest, wise, and responsible human beings who think logically, imagine creatively, communicate effectively, act with integrity, and contribute positively to their families, communities, and society.”

We envision education that goes beyond examinations and certificates, building character, wisdom, confidence, and responsible citizenship for a better future.

MISSION

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality education in a safe, disciplined, and supportive environment where every student is encouraged to:

Gain meaningful knowledge and develop a lifelong love of learning.

Build strong character based on honesty, respect, discipline, and responsibility.

Think logically and critically rather than simply memorizing information.

Develop imagination and creativity to understand and solve problems.

Communicate confidently and respectfully with others.

Develop wisdom and sound judgment to make responsible decisions.

Become responsible citizens who respect their community, country, and humanity.

Prepare for life, not merely examinations — true education is reflected in character, choices, and contribution to society.

At Al-Khair Public High School, we believe that education is complete only when knowledge is joined with character and wisdom.

FROM THE PRINCIPAL

Principal’s Message

Bahader Said
Founder & Principal

Dear Parents,

Education is not merely the achievement of a certificate or a piece of paper. True education is the process of developing a good human being, a responsible citizen, and a thoughtful individual.

At Al-Khair Public High School, we believe that every child deserves an education rooted in honesty, discipline, character, and respect. Our responsibility is not only to prepare students for examinations and careers, but also to prepare them for life.

We strive to develop students who can think logically, imagine creatively, communicate confidently, make wise decisions, and distinguish between what is right and wrong. Knowledge becomes truly valuable when it is accompanied by wisdom, integrity, and a sense of responsibility toward others and society.

We deeply value the trust parents place in our school. We therefore aim to work together with families to create an environment where students learn not only what to think, but how to think; not only how to succeed, but how to live with honesty, dignity, and purpose.

Our goal is simple: to educate the mind, strengthen the character, and prepare responsible human beings for a better society.

Bahader Said
Principal, Al-Khair Public High School
